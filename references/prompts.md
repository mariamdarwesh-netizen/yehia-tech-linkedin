# The prompts

Word for word from the Yehia Tech LinkedIn kit. `{field}` = the user's details (see SKILL.md §1); `[ ]` = material the user pastes or fills in.
★ = Yehia used this prompt on screen in the video.

## Step 1 · أول انطباع (Photo · Cover · URL)

No prompt. Use the checklist in playbook.md.

## Step 2 · سطر واحد بيبيعك (Headline)

### Headline Fix ★

What it gives: يديك 10 صيغ Headline محددة تختار منها.

```
Write 10 LinkedIn headline options for me that place me among the best people in {industry}. Each one should be specific, focused on results, and strong enough to stop someone mid-scroll.
```

### Keywords Audit

What it gives: يعرّفك الكلمات اللي الـ recruiters بيدوّروا بيها، وفين تحطها في بروفايلك.

```
You are a LinkedIn search expert. For the role of {role}, list the 25 keywords recruiters type most often when they search. Point out which ones my profile is missing and suggest where each one fits naturally.
```

### Positioning

What it gives: جملة واحدة بتقول إنت مختلف عن غيرك في إيه.

```
Look at my background and write a short positioning statement that makes me stand out as a trusted name in {industry}. It should be easy to remember, believable, and clearly different from others in my field.
```

## Step 3 · حكايتك في سطرين (About)

### الـ AI يسألك الأول ★

What it gives: لو مش عارف تبدأ: الـ AI يسألك 8 أسئلة ويكتب الـ About من إجاباتك.

```
My name is {name}. I'm aiming for {role} in {industry}.
Interview me one question at a time about my studies, activities, projects and goals. After 8 questions, write 3 versions of my LinkedIn About: storytelling, simple and human, and strong personal branding. Recommend the best one in two lines.
```

### About بأسلوب PAS

What it gives: لو عندك About: يعيد كتابته بحيث أول سطرين يشدّوا.

```
Rewrite my About section as a story whose first 2 lines grab a recruiter's attention. Follow the PAS structure (Problem, Agitate, Solution), stay under 2000 characters, and finish with a clear call to action.

My current About: [paste it here]
```

## Step 4 · ورّيهم شغلك (Experience · Featured · Recommendations)

### Experience (XYZ) ★

What it gives: يحوّل وصف شغلك من «مهام» لـ«نتايج» حقيقية.

```
Turn each of my job descriptions into achievements using the XYZ formula: "Accomplished X by doing Y, which led to Z." Only use numbers I give you, and ask me when a number is missing. Focus on the impact I made, not the tasks I was given.

My experience: [paste it here]
```

### Featured Strategy

What it gives: يقولك تثبّت إيه فوق في بروفايلك عشان يبان شغلك من أول نظرة.

```
Recommend 5 items I should pin in my Featured section to build trust quickly as a {role}. For each one, tell me what it should say and why it works.
```

### طلب توصية ★

What it gives: رسالة مهذبة تطلب بيها توصية، وتقول للشخص يركّز على إيه.

```
Write a short, warm LinkedIn message asking [person's name] for a recommendation. I'm {name}, aiming for {role}. Ask them to mention these 3 points: [point 1], [point 2], [point 3]. Under 80 words, polite, not needy.
```

## Step 5 · اختبار الـ Recruiter (Profile Audit · Recruiter Test)

### Profile Audit ★

What it gives: يقيّم بروفايلك من 10 ويقولك أسرع 3 تعديلات.

```
You are a LinkedIn profile consultant. I'm sharing my profile as a PDF plus screenshots. Review the whole profile, score it out of 10, and list the 10 changes that would bring me more profile views, connection requests, and opportunities. Put the 3 quickest fixes first.
```

### Recruiter Test ★

What it gives: يتقمّص recruiter في الشركة اللي نفسك فيها ويقولك هيكلمك ولا لأ.

```
Act as a senior recruiter at {company} who just opened my profile. Would you contact me? Explain why or why not, be completely honest, and list the 5 biggest red flags you see.
```

## Step 6 · شبكتك من الصفر (Connections)

### Outreach DM

What it gives: 3 رسايل قصيرة تبعتها لـ recruiters من غير ما تبان بتشحت شغل.

```
Write 3 short first-message templates I can send to recruiters at {companies}. Keep them personal, direct, and easy to reply to, with no corporate language.
```

### خطة 30 يوم

What it gives: تعمل إيه كل يوم لمدة شهر عشان شبكتك تكبر صح.

```
Build me a 30 day LinkedIn networking plan to connect with the people who matter in {industry}. Include what to do each day, message templates, and how to engage with their posts.
```

## Step 7 · أول بوست (Posts · Comments · Open to Work)

### كومنت بيضيف ★

What it gives: كومنتات بتخلّي الناس تشوف اسمك وتفتح بروفايلك.

```
Here is a LinkedIn post: [paste the post]. I work in {industry}. Write 3 short comments that add a real insight, an experience, or a smart question. No flattery, no emojis, under 40 words each.
```

### 30 فكرة بوست

What it gives: شهر كامل أفكار بوستات، مع أول جملة لكل بوست.

```
I create content about {topic}. Give me 30 LinkedIn post ideas that would interest recruiters, clients, and decision makers, each with a strong opening line.
```
